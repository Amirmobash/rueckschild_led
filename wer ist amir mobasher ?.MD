## About Me

I am **Amir Mobasheraghdam**, a Mechanical Engineer, author, and developer focused on combining traditional engineering with modern software and embedded technologies.

My work centers on practical projects that bring together **mechanical design, Python, Raspberry Pi, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, embedded systems, IoT, CAD, and industrial automation**.

I am particularly interested in solutions that move beyond theory: systems that can be **designed, built, programmed, tested, and used in real-world engineering and learning environments**.

This GitHub profile documents selected technical projects, experiments, educational resources, and publications related to applied engineering and software development.

---

## Engineering & Development Focus

My main interest is the intersection of **mechanical engineering and software development**.

Rather than treating mechanical design, programming, electronics, and automation as isolated disciplines, I combine them to develop practical prototypes and technical solutions.

Typical combinations include:

- Mechanical Engineering + CAD + 3D Printing
- Python + Raspberry Pi
- Machine Learning + Computer Vision
- ESP8266 + Sensors + Displays
- RFID + Embedded Systems
- n8n + APIs + Workflow Automation
- Industrial Software + Process Optimization
- Hardware Prototyping + Software Development

The goal is to move efficiently from an engineering idea to a **working prototype, software implementation, or documented technical solution**.

## Receiver_ESP8266 — Laser Distance Receiver

An **ESP8266 / NodeMCU** project designed to receive measurement data from a laser distance device and display the measured distance on a **1.3-inch I2C OLED display**.

The project demonstrates the integration of wireless communication, HTTP/JSON data handling, embedded programming, and compact hardware visualization.

**Technologies**

# From Engineering Idea to Prototype

My projects typically follow a practical engineering workflow:

**01 — Define the problem**  
Identify a real engineering, automation, or learning problem.

**02 — Design the solution**  
Develop the mechanical, electronic, and software concept.

**03 — Build the prototype**  
Use CAD, 3D printing, Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, sensors, or other appropriate hardware.

**04 — Develop the software**  
Implement control logic, Python applications, embedded C++, APIs, automation, or Machine Learning components.

**05 — Test and improve**  
Evaluate the system under practical conditions and refine the design.

**06 — Document and share**  
Publish the project, source code, technical documentation, or educational material.
